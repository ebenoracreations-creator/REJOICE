Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026 • Rejoice Events & Floral Studio, Kottayam, Kerala

At Rejoice Events & Floral Studio, we cherish the trust you place in us when inviting us to plan your most sacred life celebrations — from luxury weddings and baptisms to birthdays, memorial services, and custom private celebrations across Kerala. We treat your privacy with the utmost respect and confidentiality.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, request quotes, or submit celebration checklists, we may collect the following details that you voluntarily provide:

  • Client Contact Details: Full name, phone number, and WhatsApp number.
  • Celebration Details: Event date, estimated guest count, preferred venue or church location across Kerala, theme preferences, and customized service selections.
  • Technical Information: Device type, browser information, and referral sources collected anonymously to optimize website speed and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide exceptional, bespoke event coordination services:

  • To prepare customized cost estimates and celebration checklists based on your specific requirements.
  • To communicate directly with you via WhatsApp or phone regarding venue inspections, floral designs, stage layouts, and scheduling.
  • To coordinate logistical setup with verified vendor partners (e.g. stage construction, authentic Kerala sadya catering, audio-visual technicians) exclusively for your confirmed event.

3. Zero Third-Party Selling or Marketing Spam

We never sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal information or contact details to third-party telemarketers or external advertisers under any circumstances. Your celebration plans remain strictly confidential between you and the Rejoice Events planning team.

4. Secure Communications & WhatsApp Data

Our website utilizes industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption (HTTPS) across all pages. Checklist inquiries sent to our WhatsApp desk (+91 99614 02646) are secured through WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption protocols.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

You may at any time request the deletion, update, or verification of any personal contact information we hold. Simply reach out to us at our official contact channels below, and we will fulfill your request promptly.
